New:

```latex
Several standard algebraic identities, among them associativity,
alternativity, flexibility, power-associativity and commutativity,
fail on the order $(\LL, +, \star)$.  Each failure has a witness in the
ambient algebra that can be checked by hand, and four times that witness
lies in~$\LL$ (shown after the table).  Two observations make the check
short.  First, if $u$ and~$v$
each have a single nonzero block, $a$ in block~$\alpha$ and $b$ in
block~$\beta$, then $u \star v$ has the single nonzero block $a
\cdot_\sigma b$, placed by the routing rule of
Definition~\ref{def:triple}.  Second, $\sigma$ fixes $e_0$, $e_3$,
$e_4$ and~$e_6$, which span a subalgebra (the triple $(3,4,6)$
of~\eqref{eq:fano}), so on it $\cdot_\sigma$ is the standard product.
Write $\varepsilon_1 = (e_0, 0, 0)$, $\varepsilon_2 = (0, e_0, 0)$,
$\varepsilon_3 = (0, 0, e_0)$ and $\omega = \varepsilon_1 +
\varepsilon_2 + \varepsilon_3$.  By the first observation,
$\varepsilon_\alpha \star \varepsilon_\alpha = \varepsilon_\alpha$, and
$\varepsilon_\alpha \star \varepsilon_\beta = \varepsilon_\gamma$
whenever $\{\alpha, \beta, \gamma\} = \{1, 2, 3\}$.  The quartic
identity requires all five parenthesizations of $u \star u \star u
\star u$ to coincide, and $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ denotes the
Euclidean inner product on~$\RR^{24}$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Identity & Witness & The two sides \\
\midrule
Associativity $(u\star v)\star w = u\star(v\star w)$ &
  $\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, \varepsilon_3$ &
  $\varepsilon_3 \ne \varepsilon_1$ \\
Left alternativity $(u\star u)\star v = u\star(u\star v)$ &
  $\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2$ &
  $\varepsilon_3 \ne \varepsilon_2$ \\
Right alternativity $(u\star v)\star v = u\star(v\star v)$ &
  $\varepsilon_2, \varepsilon_1$ &
  $\varepsilon_2 \ne \varepsilon_3$ \\
Flexibility $(u\star v)\star u = u\star(v\star u)$ &
  $(e_3, e_4, 0), \varepsilon_1$ &
  differ by $(0, -2e_6, 2e_6)$ \\
Cube power-associativity $(u\star u)\star u = u\star(u\star u)$ &
  $(e_0, e_3, e_4)$ &
  differ by $(0, -4e_6, 4e_6)$ \\
Quartic power-associativity &
  $\varepsilon_1 - \varepsilon_2$ &
  $3\,u\star u \ne -3\,u\star u$ \\
Commutativity $u\star v = v\star u$ &
  $(e_3, 0, 0), (e_4, 0, 0)$ &
  $(e_6, 0, 0) \ne (-e_6, 0, 0)$ \\
Norm multiplicativity $\Nrm(u\star v) = \Nrm(u)\Nrm(v)$ &
  $\omega, \varepsilon_1 - \varepsilon_2$ &
  $0 \ne 6$ \\
Symmetric composition $\langle u\star v, w\rangle =
  \langle u, v\star w\rangle$ &
  $\varepsilon_1, (e_3, 0, 0), (e_3, 0, 0)$ &
  $1 \ne -1$ \\
Multiplicative identity &
  \multicolumn{2}{l@{}}{none exists (Remark~\ref{rem:no-identity})} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
For flexibility and the cube identity, the table gives the left side
minus the right side.  For the quartic identity, the two sides shown are $((u \star u)
\star u) \star u$ and $(u \star u) \star (u \star u)$: with $u =
\varepsilon_1 - \varepsilon_2$, $u \star u = \varepsilon_1 +
\varepsilon_2 - 2\varepsilon_3$ and $(u \star u) \star u = 3u = u
\star (u \star u)$, so the cube identity holds at this~$u$.  Each
identity is homogeneous in its arguments, so multiplying a witness
by~$4$ multiplies both sides by the same power of~$4$.  The scaled
witness lies in $(2L)^3 \subseteq \LL$, since $4e_i \in 2L$ and $2L
\subseteq L\bar s \cap Ls$ (Section~\ref{subsec:wilson-leech}), so the
identities fail on~$\LL$ itself.\footnote{\scriptref{verify\_identity\_witnesses.py}.}
Appendix~\ref{app:samples} estimates by sampling how often the
identities from left alternativity to symmetric composition hold on
minimal vectors.

The failure of norm multiplicativity rules out $(\LL, +, \star)$
being a composition algebra.
```
