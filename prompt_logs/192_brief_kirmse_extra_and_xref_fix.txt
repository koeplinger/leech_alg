New §2 added: "Kirmse 1924 -- two further contributions worth
crediting". Covers:

(a) Nascent ideal theory on the E_8 lattice. Kirmse develops an
    ideal-theoretic treatment of integral octonions, an early
    substantive companion contribution to the multiplicative-
    closure question; first such development on an E_8-type
    lattice. Mahler 1942 takes up this line again later with the
    Euclidean property and principal-ideal theorem, but Kirmse's
    earlier work in this direction is underrepresented in v4's
    Kirmse paragraph.

(b) Algebra alternativity as foundational building block. Kirmse
    methodically takes alternativity as a basic axiom (not derived
    from full associativity), suggesting a programme of replacing
    associativity by alternativity at the foundation of integral-
    octonion arithmetic. This anticipates the structural role
    alternativity plays in Cayley--Dickson algebras and their
    integral arithmetic in later literature.
