Prompt 260 -- Proof-reading question: why "twisted octonion algebra" in 5.3?
Date: 2026-07-18

User: 'Help me understand this change: [the Section 5.3 wording "Every
automorphism is a blockwise automorphism of the twisted octonion algebra
(O, .sigma) of Definition 3.2"] Why are you referring to the _twisted_
octonion algebra? Is this because the explicit demonstration of C_6 uses the
sigma(1,2)-twisted version of the standard representation given at the
beginning? Or do you want to imply a deeper reason?'

ANSWER (the first reading; no deeper implication):
The triple product star is assembled from ._sigma in its nine blocks, so a
blockwise map u_alpha -> A(u_alpha) commutes with star exactly when A
preserves ._sigma, i.e. A in Aut(O, ._sigma). The displayed C_6 generator is
such an A. Verified again this session on all 64 basis pairs:
    A6 preserves the STANDARD product:  False
    A6 preserves the TWISTED product:   True
    sigma A6 sigma preserves the STANDARD product: True
So writing plain "octonion automorphism" would send a checking reader to test
A6 against the standard Fano table of Section 2.1, where it FAILS -- that was
review finding M2 (prompt 245).

Nothing deeper: Aut(O, ._sigma) = sigma Aut(O, .) sigma is a conjugate copy of
the same compact G_2 inside O(8). One could equivalently display the
conjugated generator sigma A6 sigma and call it an automorphism of the
standard product, but then the blockwise action on star would carry the
sigma-conjugation instead. In the coordinates the paper fixes (sigma = (1 2)
throughout), the self-consistent statement is the one printed.
