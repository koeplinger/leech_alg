  Definition 3.1 (Transposition twist).
  Let sigma = (i j) be a transposition on {1,...,7}, extended to the
  linear involution sigma : R^8 -> R^8 that fixes e_0 and swaps
  e_i <-> e_j. The sigma-twisted product on O is
       x .sigma y := sigma(sigma(x) . sigma(y)),
  where . is the standard octonion product.

  Proposition 3.2.
  sigma : (O, .) -> (O, .sigma) is an algebra isomorphism:
  sigma(x.y) = sigma(x) .sigma sigma(y) for all x, y in O.
  Consequently .sigma is composition: N(x .sigma y) = N(x) N(y).

  Proof. sigma(x).sigma sigma(y) = sigma(sigma^2(x) . sigma^2(y))
  = sigma(x.y), since sigma^2 = id.
