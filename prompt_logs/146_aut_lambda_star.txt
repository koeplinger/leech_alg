Prompt 146 -- Aut(Lambda, +, star) probe
Date: 2026-05-23

User: "Since we have GAP, can we try to answer this open question?
'The automorphism group of (Lambda, +, star) and its relationship to the
Conway group Co_0.'"

Approach: bound Aut(Lambda, +, star) by testing candidate elements of
Co_0 = Aut(Lambda, +). Candidates that DO preserve star -> in
Aut(Lambda, +, star); candidates that DO NOT -> excluded.

First-step candidates to test in exact arithmetic (Python, using the
existing 24-vector Z-basis of Lambda and the star machinery):

1. Z_3 block-cyclic permutation: (x,y,z) -> (y,z,x). Built-in symmetry,
   expected to preserve star.
2. S_3 transposition of blocks, e.g., (x,y,z) -> (y,x,z). Check
   whether the full S_3 symmetry holds, or only the Z_3 subgroup.
3. Componentwise negation -I_{24}: trivially in Aut(Lambda, +); does
   NOT preserve star (bilinearity: g(u).g(v) = u.v, but g(u.v) = -u.v).
   Confirms Aut(Lambda, +, star) is a proper subgroup of Co_0.
4. sigma block-wise: (sigma(x), sigma(y), sigma(z)). May not even
   preserve Lambda (sigma moves Ls).
5. Optional: diagonal octonion automorphism g applied to each block,
   for various g in the finite subgroup of G_2 preserving L = D_8^+.

GAP step (later, if the first step is informative): use AtlasRep to
load Co_0 as 24x24 integer matrices, then characterise the subgroup
preserving the star tensor.

Status: in progress.
