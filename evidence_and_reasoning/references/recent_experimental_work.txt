Topic: Recent computer-assisted experimental work in adjacent areas
=========================================================

This file collects references for recent works, in lattice theory and in
nonassociative algebra, that pursue related questions through computer-assisted
experiment. They are cited in Section 6 (Related work) of the paper as adjacent
experimental efforts, not as prior art the present construction builds on. All
bibliographic fields were verified against live sources (journal pages, arXiv,
author publication lists) before inclusion.

---

### [KirschmerNebe2022]

- **Authors**: Markus Kirschmer, Gabriele Nebe
- **Title**: Binary Hermitian lattices over number fields
- **Published in**: Experimental Mathematics, Volume 31, Issue 1, 2022, pages 280–301
- **Year**: 2022
- **DOI**: 10.1080/10586458.2019.1618756
- **Source**: Not redistributed locally (Taylor & Francis copyright).
  Canonical DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.2019.1618756
- **Topics**: leech_lattice, algorithms
- **Originality note**: Published in a peer-reviewed journal (Experimental
  Mathematics). No dispute over priority or originality known to this project.
- **Notes**: Classifies binary Hermitian lattices over number fields by computer,
  with an application to the classification of 48-dimensional extremal even
  unimodular lattices admitting a semilarge automorphism. Cited as computational
  lattice theory in the target venue.

### [Nebe2012]

- **Authors**: Gabriele Nebe
- **Title**: An even unimodular 72-dimensional lattice of minimum 8
- **Published in**: Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (Crelle),
  Volume 673, 2012, pages 237–247
- **Year**: 2012
- **DOI**: 10.1515/crelle.2011.175
- **Preprint**: arXiv:1008.2862 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2862)
- **Source**: Not redistributed locally (De Gruyter copyright); preprint public on arXiv.
- **Topics**: leech_lattice, e8_lattice
- **Originality note**: Published in a peer-reviewed journal (Crelle). No dispute
  over priority or originality known to this project.
- **Notes**: Constructs the first known extremal even unimodular 72-dimensional
  lattice of minimum 8, as a tensor product of the Barnes lattice and the Leech
  lattice over the ring of integers of Q(sqrt(-7)). Cited as a Leech-derived
  construction alongside the Kirschmer–Nebe experimental work.

### [HohnMason2016]

- **Authors**: Gerald Höhn, Geoffrey Mason
- **Title**: The 290 fixed-point sublattices of the Leech lattice
- **Published in**: Journal of Algebra, Volume 448, 2016, pages 618–637
- **Year**: 2016
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.08.028
- **Preprint**: arXiv:1505.06420 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.06420)
- **Source**: Not redistributed locally (Elsevier copyright); preprint public on arXiv.
- **Topics**: leech_lattice
- **Originality note**: Published in a peer-reviewed journal (Journal of Algebra,
  Elsevier). No dispute over priority or originality known to this project.
- **Notes**: Determines, by computer, the 290 orbits of fixed-point sublattices of
  the Leech lattice under the action of the Conway group Co_0, with the
  corresponding coinvariant lattices and stabilizing subgroups. Directly relevant
  as a computational census of Leech sublattices.

### [HohnMason2023]

- **Authors**: Gerald Höhn, Geoffrey Mason
- **Title**: Most vertex superalgebras associated to an odd unimodular lattice of
  rank 24 have an N=4 superconformal structure
- **Published in**: Experimental Mathematics, Volume 32, Issue 3, 2023, pages 502–504
- **Year**: 2023
- **DOI**: 10.1080/10586458.2021.1980458
- **Preprint**: arXiv:1810.00254 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00254)
- **Source**: Not redistributed locally (Taylor & Francis copyright); preprint public on arXiv.
- **Topics**: leech_lattice, background
- **Originality note**: Published in a peer-reviewed journal (Experimental
  Mathematics). No dispute over priority or originality known to this project.
- **Notes**: Establishes, by computer, that at least 267 of the 273 vertex
  superalgebras associated to odd unimodular lattices of rank 24 carry an N=4
  superconformal subalgebra. Cited as experimental work in the target venue on
  rank-24 unimodular lattices.

### [HohnSeysen2025]

- **Authors**: Gerald Höhn, Martin Seysen
- **Title**: The order of the Monster finite simple group
- **Published in**: Preprint (arXiv), 2025
- **Year**: 2025
- **Preprint**: arXiv:2508.01037 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01037)
- **Source**: Preprint public on arXiv.
- **Topics**: background, algorithms
- **Originality note**: Preprint, not yet in a peer-reviewed journal at the time of
  writing. No dispute over priority or originality known to this project.
- **Notes**: A self-contained, computer-assisted determination of the order of the
  Monster (and the Baby Monster), by counting idempotent axes in the Griess
  algebra using the mmgroup software package. Cited as computer-assisted work by
  the same group.
