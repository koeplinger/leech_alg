The overlap to credit:

Baez/Egan use the "key lemma" L_1 ∩ L_2 = 2 L_0 (per the original
verification PDF §4) as a step in their geometric Leech-lattice
theorem. Wilson (2009, Section 2) has the identical identity in his
octonion-specific setup: L\bar s ∩ Ls = 2 L. These are the same
algebraic fact in different notations.

What we do that Baez/Egan did not: descend the octonion product
modulo 2L to an F_2-bilinear product mu-bar on L/2L, identify
V = sigma(L\bar s)/2L as a (two-sided) ideal and W = sigma(Ls)/2L
as a subalgebra, and under the natural plus-type quadratic form
recognize L/2L = V ⊕ W as a Witt decomposition into complementary
Lagrangians.
