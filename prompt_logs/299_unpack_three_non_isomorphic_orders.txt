Answered in the conversation (no file changed): the sentence lists three
products on the same lattice Lambda, each of which closes, so each makes
(Lambda, +, star_g) a ring (an order): star_sigma = star (span index 2^16),
star_pi for any even closing permutation pi (2^10), star_rho (1).  A ring
isomorphism f between two of them is an additive bijection of Lambda with
f(u star_g v) = f(u) star_h f(v); it maps products onto products, hence
the span S_g onto S_h, and so [Lambda : S_g] = [Lambda : S_h].  Different
indices, so no two of the three are isomorphic.  The invariant gives a
lower bound only: equal indices (e.g. the odd closing permutations, all
2^16) do not make orders isomorphic.  One clearer wording was offered,
not applied.
