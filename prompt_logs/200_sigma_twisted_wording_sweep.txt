sigma-twisted octonion product; this isn't a fair description (it's
simply an octonion product, and we show by means of the sigma twist

consistency sweep."

The state-of-the-art framing (per the paper v5 and the user's
clarification here):
- The product used in the construction is SIMPLY AN OCTONION PRODUCT
  (one of the octonion products on R^8; all are isomorphic).
- The sigma twist is the DEVICE by which we show how this octonion
  product fits with Wilson's representation of the Leech lattice
  (Wilson's representation fixes a particular standard product; sigma
  relates ours to it).
- So describing the construction's product as "the sigma-twisted
  octonion product" over-privileges the twist: the twist is about the
  fit with a chosen representation, not about the product being some
  exotic twisted object.
