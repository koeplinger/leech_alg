## Section 5.6: other twists, closure and span of the image (`sec:census`, `sec:span`; v7: Sections 5.5 and 5.6)

- **What changed.**  v7's Sections 5.5, "Span of the image", and 5.6, "Closure of permutation cycles", are one subsection, "Other twists: closure and span of the image", which carries both labels.  In order:
  - What the subsection tests: $\sigma$ replaced by other maps, whether the triple product still closes on $\Lambda$, and how much of $\Lambda$ its products reach.
  - A *twist*: an invertible linear map $g$ of $\mathbb R^8$ with $g(L) = L$, with the twisted product $x \cdot_g y := g^{-1}(g(x) \cdot g(y))$ and the triple product $\star_g$ of Definition 3.3 with $\cdot_g$ in all nine block products, so that $\star_\sigma = \star$.
